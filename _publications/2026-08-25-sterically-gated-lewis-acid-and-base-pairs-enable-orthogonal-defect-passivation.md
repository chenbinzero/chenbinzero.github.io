Lewis acids and Lewis bases each passivate one polarity of charged point defect in perovskites, but combined they react into quenching adducts that passivate nothing. This work identifies chemically orthogonal families &ndash; bulky sp<sup>3</sup>-phosphine bases that cannot react with fluorinated aromatic acids &ndash; whose steric gating suppresses acid&ndash;base orbital overlap while leaving surface defect sites accessible to each passivator, giving additive trap suppression and inverted solar cells with 27.4% power conversion efficiency (certified steady-state 25.8%).
